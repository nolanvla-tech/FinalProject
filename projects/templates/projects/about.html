About Me

I'm a Business Fellow at Baylor University with a passion for strategy, venture investing, and problem-solving. I combine financial analysis with technology to drive meaningful business decisions.

My Journey

As a triple major student in Finance, Economics, and Management Information Systems at Baylor University, I've developed a unique perspective on business strategy and technology. My experience spans from venture capital analysis at the Baylor Angel Network to financial analysis at Dell Technologies to IT implementations at Southwest Solutions Group.

What drives me is the intersection of finance, strategy, and technology. I'm fascinated by how data and insights can inform better business decisions, whether it's pricing optimization, M&A strategy, or early-stage venture evaluation.

I'll be joining KPMG in August 2026 as a Strategy Technology M&A Associate in Chicago, where I'll help clients navigate complex strategic and technology-driven transactions.

Beyond academics and work, I'm passionate about sustainability (I co-founded Baylor's first coordinated move-out donation program), consulting (Director of Recruitment at The Consulting Group at Baylor), and learning (cooking, hiking, and creative pursuits).
